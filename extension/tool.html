Guide and how it works

Is your audio loud enough, or too loud?

Drop in a video or audio file. The tool measures integrated loudness (LUFS), true peak and loudness range, then shows what YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and broadcast will do with it. Nothing is uploaded.

Everything runs inside this tab. Your files are not uploaded, sent or stored, and the page is blocked from making network requests.

Drop a video or audio file

MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3, WAV or M4A that your browser can play. The sound is measured on this device.

Loudness

Measured with the ITU-R BS.1770-4 method that streaming platforms and broadcasters use. Integrated loudness is the one number to compare against a target.

Against platform levels

What each platform is generally reported to do with a file at this loudness. Platforms change their rules, so check the one you publish on.

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok do not publish a loudness target that I could verify, so they are not listed. A common practice is to deliver social video at about -14 LUFS with the true peak at or below -1 dBTP. Reference levels were last checked on 26 November 2026 from secondary sources, not each platform's own page.

Fix it
